# LaneCue Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 9 September 2026**

LaneCue respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the LaneCue mobile application and how that information is used.

## Information LaneCue Uses

### Location Data

LaneCue uses location information when you give the app permission to access your location.

Precise and/or approximate location may be used to:

- Show your current position
- Provide routes and navigation
- Provide location-based driving guidance
- Support the operation and reliability of the app
- Help analyse and improve app performance

Location data is not used for advertising and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

### Search Information

When you search for a destination or place in LaneCue, the search information may be processed in order to provide search results, routes and navigation.

Search information is not used for advertising, is not used for cross-app tracking and is not intentionally linked to your identity by LaneCue.

### Identifiers

LaneCue does not require users to create an account.

Third-party technology used by LaneCue may use pseudonymous identifiers where necessary to provide app functionality and analytics.

These identifiers are not used by LaneCue for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Third-Party Services

LaneCue uses Mapbox technology for services including maps, location search, routing and navigation.

Information required to provide these services may be processed by Mapbox in accordance with its own privacy policy:

https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy

## Advertising and Tracking

LaneCue:

- Does not display third-party advertising
- Does not sell users' personal information
- Does not use personal information for advertising
- Does not track users across other companies' apps or websites

## Data Storage

LaneCue does not require a personal user profile.

Some information needed for app functionality may be stored locally on your device.

Third-party service providers may process or retain information in accordance with their own privacy policies and legal requirements.

## Your Choices

You control whether LaneCue can access your location through your device settings.

On iPhone, location permissions can be changed at any time in:

**Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → LaneCue**

Disabling location access may prevent navigation and location-based features from working correctly.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LaneCue develops or if legal, technical or App Store requirements change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
